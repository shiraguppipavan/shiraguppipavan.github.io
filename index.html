I own the platform layer: AWS cloud architecture, agent runtime and deployment, CI/CD, security, and observability.

Before that, as a Watsonx Platform SME at IBM, I delivered air-gapped LLM platforms on Red Hat OpenShift and GenAI engagements contributing to $10M+ in revenue, and led a 12-person team training agentic tool-calling models for Apple and Anthropic.

I started in industrial robotics in Osaka, which shaped how I build: systems have to work in the real world, every day. As a bilingual engineer I've spent years bridging teams and clients across Japan, India, and the US.

Challenge

Apple and Anthropic needed highly accurate models for agentic tool calling. Existing models showed loopholes and inconsistencies in tool-call execution, leading to unreliable agent behaviour in production environments.

Solution

Led a team of 12 to train models specifically for agentic tool calling. Identified and addressed loopholes and inconsistencies in tool-call patterns through targeted training. Benchmarked the trained models in Claude Code to validate performance and measure real-world accuracy.

Approach

How I work

A predictable path from problem to platform, with evaluation and operability built in from day one.

01

Discover

Deep-dive into the business problem before touching tooling. I map stakeholder needs, data realities, and latency constraints to define what "done" actually means.

02

Architect

Design for failure from day one. I choose boring infrastructure where possible: solid vector stores, proven orchestration, observable pipelines. Innovation comes only where it creates a real competitive edge.

03

Deliver

Ship to production with evaluation harnesses, rollback plans, and monitoring dashboards pre-built. A model in prod with no evals is a liability, and I make sure clients are never left holding one.

04

Operate

Production is where value is proven. I hand over run-books, dashboards, and cost visibility, and keep evaluation running so quality holds as usage grows.
